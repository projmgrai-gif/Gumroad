Technique 24: Decision Modelling

Knowledge Area: Strategy Analysis

What it is

A technique for describing repeatable business decisions by breaking them into the questions being asked, the logic that answers them, and the underlying business rules — visualized as a decision table or diagram so multiple decision paths are easy to compare.

When to reach for it

Use it when a decision recurs often enough to standardize, involves several conditions or business rules, or needs to be handed to developers or automation as unambiguous logic. Skip it for one-off, judgment-heavy calls with no repeatable pattern.

Worked example

A subscription service needs to decide refund eligibility. Decision Modelling turns “it depends” into a table of conditions (days since purchase, usage level, prior refunds) mapped to a clear outcome for every combination — ready to hand to engineering.

Ready-to-use AI prompt

Role: You are a senior business analyst experienced in Decision Modelling (DMN-style). Context: [Paste the business decision, the conditions it depends on, and any known business rules.] Constraints: Do not invent conditions or outcomes I haven’t provided. List any missing information as open questions instead of guessing. Task: Produce a decision table with every condition combination mapped to an outcome, plus a one-paragraph summary of the underlying business rule.
