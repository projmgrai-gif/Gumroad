You don't start from a blank page

The same five-step loop, every reporting cycle — 10–15 minutes once familiar, instead of 30–45 minutes from scratch.

Dump your raw notes → set the RAG status yourself → run it through the matching AI prompt → edit for accuracy, not just tone → send it, then reuse it as your meeting agenda backbone.

Call it Green, Amber, or Red the same way — every time

The single biggest inconsistency in new-PM reporting is RAG status set by gut feel. The kit's cheat sheet fixes that.

GREEN · On Track

Scope, schedule & budget within tolerance. No open risk needs stakeholder action this period.

AMBER · At Risk

A named risk could cause a miss if unaddressed. A mitigation plan exists and is already in motion.

RED · Off Track

A commitment has already been missed, or will be without an immediate decision from outside the team.

“Could I defend this rating to someone skeptical, using only facts, not feelings?”

Sandbagging (always reporting worse than reality) and happy-ears reporting (always Green until it suddenly isn't) both destroy trust. The cheat sheet keeps status moving in small, honest increments — and AI only ever helps you word it. You still set it.

Questions, answered

Which AI tool do I need?

None specifically — the prompts are plain text and work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or your internal corporate AI tool.

Will AI just make up numbers in my report?

The workflow is built to prevent that: you set the RAG status yourself, AI only helps word it, and the checklist has you verify every date, number, and name before sending.

What format is the kit delivered in?

A single downloadable PDF — prompts, templates, cheat sheet, and checklist all in one place, ready to copy and paste.

I'm not brand new to PM — is this still useful?

Yes — it's framed for new PMs, but the templates, RAG discipline, and escalation prompts hold up for anyone who reports status regularly.
