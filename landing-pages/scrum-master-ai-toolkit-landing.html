What Scrum prescribes — and what teams adapt

Scrum itself is a small, fixed framework. This toolkit is explicit about which practices are required and which are optional — so you can defend your choices with confidence.

Element Status
Sprint + the four formal eventsRequired
Backlog refinementHelpful practice, not a formal event
Definition of Ready, story points, velocityOptional team practices
Burndown/burnup charts, DEEP, 5 WhysOptional techniques — interpret with context

Built for judgment, not autopilot

These prompts are starting points, not finished deliverables — especially anything involving specific people, sensitive conflicts, or organizational politics.

  • Don't paste confidential, personal, or regulated information into an unapproved AI system
  • Validate facts and stakeholder-facing language before using AI output
  • State known facts separately from assumptions
  • You, the Product Owner, and the Developers remain accountable — not the AI
