The 5-part pattern behind every prompt

Learn this once and you can write reliable prompts for deliverables not even covered in this guide.

  1. 01 Role — tell the AI what kind of expert to act as
  2. 02 Context — project type, industry, methodology
  3. 03 Input — your real notes, not just a topic
  4. 04 Format — the exact structure or sections you need
  5. 05 Constraints — what to avoid, or not invent

Higher-precision output, on purpose

Delimiters, chain-of-thought, and few-shot examples — three techniques layered into the prompts wherever they matter most.

The guardrail running through every prompt

AI structures and sanity-checks your material — it never invents your budget numbers, your stakeholders, or your risks. Prompts are written to flag missing input and ask before assuming, not guess.

The real skill: chaining, not memorizing

Most real PM work needs more than one deliverable from the same source material. Paste your notes into the Risk Register prompt, feed that output into the Communications Plan prompt, then feed both into your next Status Report — treating each deliverable as an input to the next, the way real project work actually flows.
