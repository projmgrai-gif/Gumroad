See exactly what you're getting

Every question comes with full rationale — including why each wrong answer is wrong, not just why the right one is right. Here's an actual question from Section 3 of the guide.

Section 3 · Q7 (Scenario) Domain II, Task 5

A supplier proposes a change to their approach that would improve the deliverable's quality but isn't part of the original contract scope. How should the PM handle this?

AApprove the change immediately without evaluating cost or schedule impact
BEvaluate the proposed change's impact on cost, schedule, and contract terms, and route it through the appropriate change process before approving it — an improvement is still a change to what was agreed
CReject the proposal automatically, since it wasn't part of the original scope
DIgnore the proposal entirely without a response
Show full rationale

Answer: B — Evaluating cost/schedule impact and routing the proposal through the appropriate change process is the sound response — an improvement outside the original contract scope is still a change to what was agreed, so approving it unevaluated (A) skips necessary process, rejecting it automatically (C) may lose a genuine improvement, and ignoring it entirely (D) fails to address a legitimate proposal. (This is distinct from reprioritizing an already-agreed backlog within an adaptive contract's existing capacity or scope — that kind of adjustment may be handled through the project's normal governance rather than a formal contract amendment; this question concerns a change to the contract's own scope, which does warrant the change process regardless of development approach.)

No — this guide focuses on one task, Domain II Task 5, inside a larger series covering the full July 2026 ECO across all three domains. Treat it as one focused component of a broader study plan, not standalone exam prep.

No. This is an independent study guide built directly from PMI's published July 2026 Examination Content Outline. It's not sponsored, endorsed by, or affiliated with PMI. Official ECO wording is quoted and cited where used; everything else is the author's own instructional interpretation, clearly labeled as such throughout.

This guide covers verifying vendor performance and objectives during and at the end of an active engagement. Final acceptance, contract close-out, and transition activities fall under Task 10 (manage project closure), which this series covers in its own dedicated guide.
