Every question comes with full rationale — including why each wrong answer is wrong, not just why the right one is right. Here's an actual question from Section 3 of the guide.

Section 3 · Q7 (Scenario) Domain III, Task 2

A PM discovers that a required safety inspection was skipped due to schedule pressure. The work covered by that inspection has already proceeded. What should the PM do?

AContinue without addressing the missed inspection, since the work already proceeded
BAssess the actual risk created by the missed inspection, escalate as appropriate given the severity, and determine the necessary corrective action — which might include a retroactive inspection, remediation, or other steps — rather than treating the gap as resolved simply because time has passed
CBlame the team responsible for the schedule pressure and take no further action
DCancel all future inspections, since this one was already skipped
Show full rationale

Answer: B — Assessing actual risk, escalating appropriately, and determining necessary corrective action is the sound response even though the work already proceeded — continuing without addressing the gap (A) leaves real risk unaddressed, blaming without action (C) doesn't resolve the actual gap, and canceling all future inspections (D) is a disproportionate overreaction.
